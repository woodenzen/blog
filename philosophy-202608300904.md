title: Philosophy

To me, philosophy is the continuous practice of being curious about life. It is a way of being in the world while noticing what is actually happening. Rather than merely being a body of ideas. Philosophy guides me to notice my thoughts, feelings, and actions from various perspectives. Applied to this moment, not some other moment. 

	•	Philosophy is a continuous act renewed at each instant, an orientation of attention rather than merely a body of ideas.
	
	•	Life keeps ringing the bell, reminding me to notice sooner, soften faster and return to the body and this moment again and again. Returning through touch, pressure, temperature and movement rather than another sentence in the head.
