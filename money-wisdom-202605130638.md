1. Does this purchase save time or take time?
   - Spend money to save time through outsourcing or automation.
   - Ask "Will this remove or add friction to my life?"
   - Use captured time to create more personal experiences.
2. Doing bets, having.
  - Experience bet things.
  - Experiences become stories we tell ourselves about ourselves. 
  - Experiences invite reflection.
3. People matter. Happiness is a social phenomenon.
  - Spending on others is a direct path to happiness.
  - Seeing the impact of our spending on others improves our own happiness.
4. Watch for hedonic adaptation
  - Structure as an occasional treat.
  - If everything is special, nothing is special.
  5. Spend money now to get the anticipation of the next thing.
    - Anticipation is a form of pleasure
    - Separate the pain of paying from the pleasure of the purchase.
    - Front-load the cost and back-load the pleasure.

Science-based tips on how to spend money to increase utility and happiness. From Dan Pink.
