#### Reference
Shobogenzo Bodaisatta Shishobo

#### Question
How does Zen view love, both in classical literature and in contemporary practice? 

- Love starts in the small weather system in the chest, annoyed, horny, grieving, hungry.
- Love is intimacy without ownership.
- Don't add perfume to the present-moment experience of washing the bowl.
- When the self relaxes its fists, love is what hands do. They cook rice, change sheets, bow, write the note, touch the shoulder, apologize before the ego has finished its courtroom speech.
- A fresh gesture is always possible in each moment. Noticing this is the first step in the path of love.
- The Zen answer is: sit down, shut up kindly, listen, then stand up and help
- Zen love is letting the doing of things shape your life. Let each moment become what it becomes through causes, conditions, timing, tone, body, need, and love. 
- Give something. Say one kind sentence. Do one useful act. Join the other person’s world for ten seconds. That is practice with sawdust on it.
