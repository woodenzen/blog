title: Reflecting on a Reflection Practice
---

My journaling practice is to reflect on my experience. Some days with greater depth than others. Lately, I've been wondering whether all this reflection changes how I enter the next moment. Everything happens before what happens next. This includes reflection. Does reflecting on experience change how I enter the next moment? Yes, it does. But how and by what nature? Reflection can take different forms. The spectrum of reflection forms, from analytical to contemplative, shapes how the next moment manifests.

	•	Analytical Reflection without becoming an endless explanation.
	•	Imaginative and anticipatory reflection without scripting the future.
	•	Dialogical reflection with another person.
	•	Ethical and affectionate reflection
	•	Embodied reflection - dancing with the phenomena of the moment. Looking closely at moment-to-moment experience. 
	•	Reflection on thinking itself or meta-reflection.
	•	Receptive contemplation - a dropping away of reflection, allowing surprise and delight.
