Freewriting. John Cage. I want to study his life more and gain inspiration from it. I had almost forgotten about him until this morning's Hypomnemata entry, "I have nothing to say and I am saying it and that is poetry as I need it." This struck me as a way to get into more note-taking. This attitude or position of having nothing to say and saying it with style is all I can do. 

It doesn't matter how I do this; just do it. Maybe it does matter how I do this. There might be a particular way to do it. The discovery is the doing. 

The writing is my own. The thinking is my own. The discovery is my own. Can AI play any role? Help with syntax, grammar and spelling. It can suggest improvements in sentence structure. If I ask, it can provide ideas to improve the flow of idea exploration. It is like having an editor, something I can't find in my life. My help. Help, but not control. 

John Cage led a full life. I should read some of the papers written about his life. Pick a few of those that intersect with Zen or Love. John Cage was a polymath. Music, poetry, dance, Zen. I wonder how many and how diverse one's interests have to be to be considered a polymath? What level of expertise in each is required? 

Here I am, a fool pretending to be an intellectual. Or am I an intellectual pretending to be a fool? I do know that my potential is hidden, underexposed.
